May was a big month. Here's what shipped.

## New tools

**[API Assertion Builder](/tools/api-assertion-builder)** — paste a JSON response, get typed assertions for Jest, Playwright, pytest, or Supertest. Three modes: exact values, type-safe, and schema-only.

**[Form Test Data Generator](/tools/form-test-data-generator)** — build a form schema, get a full test matrix per field: valid, invalid, boundary, XSS payloads, SQL injection, Unicode. Export as CSV, JSON, or Markdown.

**[HTML Minifier](/tools/html-minifier)** and **[HTML Beautifier](/tools/html-beautifier)** — formatting both ways for markup that came from somewhere else and needs cleaning up before you can work with it.

**[JavaScript Minifier](/tools/javascript-minifier)** and **[CSS Minifier](/tools/css-minifier)** — minify and beautify, with download.

## New category: QA & Testing

The [QA & Testing category](/tools/qa-testing) is now live, with the Form Test Data Generator as its anchor tool. More tools coming.

## Homepage redesign

The homepage was rebuilt with a two-column hero, a "Popular tools" section, and a "Browse by category" grid. It's faster to navigate and better at surfacing what ToolKit actually does.
